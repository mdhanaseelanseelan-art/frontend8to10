BMW

The iconic BMW logo represents the company's aviation roots, featuring the blue and white quadrants of the Bavarian flag which symbolize a spinning propeller against a clear sky. Known for its performance-oriented heritage—including the legendary 'M' division—BMW balances its traditional combustion engine lineup with a heavy push toward electromobility, hydrogen fuel cells, and advanced AI-driven innovations.

YAMAHA

Yamaha is a massive Japanese multinational brand that operates primarily through two independent entities: Yamaha Corporation (which manufactures musical instruments and audio equipment) and Yamaha Motor Company (which produces motorcycles, marine vehicles, and industrial machinery). Yamaha Corporation Originally founded in 1887 as Nippon Gakki Co., Ltd. by Torakusu Yamaha, it started as a reed organ and piano manufacturer. Today, it is the world's largest manufacturer of musical instruments and is renowned globally for its synthesizers, acoustic instruments, and professional audio gear.
