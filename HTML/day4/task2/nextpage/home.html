Companies in Chennai, Bangalore & Hyderabad

Chennai is a premier global hub for both IT services and software products, heavily concentrated in tech corridors like OMR (Old Mahabalipuram Road) and DLF IT Park.

The city hosts some of the world's most successful tech product companies alongside multi-billion dollar enterprise service providers, making it a critical talent capital in South Asia.

Top Service-Based Providers

Tech Mahindra: Specializes in IT services and customer support with large delivery centers across Chennai (major campuses in RMZ Millennia and DLF IT Park).

Concentrix (formerly Concentrix & Webhelp): Headquartered in Chennai; a major global BPO specializing in customer support and experience management.

Teleperformance (TP): A multinational BPO operating multiple centers across Chennai, offering omnichannel customer service.

Amazon Development Centre: A massive employer in Chennai’s Ramanujan IT City (Perungudi), hiring for massive global customer and retail business support operations.

Top Product-Based Software

Zoho Corporation: Headquartered locally in Guduvanchery, this tech giant develops a suite of cloud-based business and SaaS products.

Freshworks: Located in the RMZ Millenia IT Park; pioneers in customer service and IT management software products.

Chargebee: Operating out of Perungudi, offering advanced subscription billing and revenue management products.

Kissflow: Based in Chennai; a major player in low-code and workflow automation products.
