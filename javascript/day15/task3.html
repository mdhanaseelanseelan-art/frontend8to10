ABOUT INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY

Information technology (IT) is the use of computers, networks, and storage devices to create, process, store, and exchange electronic data. [1] (https://www.techtarget.com/it-infrastructure/definition/information-technology-IT)Core Components of ITHardware: Physical tools like computers, servers, and routers.Software: Programs and operating systems that run on hardware.Networking: Systems that connect devices to share data.Data Management: Tools used to store and organize information safely.
